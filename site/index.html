The tools

There's more to a novel
than the manuscript.

Characters to remember. Events to line up. Notes that need a home. garret keeps them alongside the writing.

Keep the story straight.

Build a story bible for your world, notes, and continuity rules. Give your characters profiles, aliases, and pictures, and follow their appearances across scenes.

Keep scene synopses and research files with the book. Use outlines and cards to work on its structure.

Know what happens when.

Lay out events on a timeline, with separate character and story tracks. Link events to scenes so you can move between the sequence and the manuscript.

Revision

Work with your editor.

Send a DOCX review document out and import the returned changes with attribution. Accept or reject proposals inside garret.

Keep comments attached to the text. Organize revisions into passes and tasks, and compare earlier scene versions when you need to look back.

Publishing

Prepare the book.

Export to Markdown, DOCX, or EPUB, or make a PDF proof copy. Set up book design, front and back matter, covers, and pen names before exporting.

Offline, no account required

Your books stay
on your disk.

Write without an account or an internet connection. Keep your books as local files, in a folder you choose.

Recovery copies, scene history, a readable mirror folder, and salvage tools help you recover work. Encrypted portable archives include a separate recovery key.

Every feature is free. There are no subscriptions or paid unlocks. garret is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later.

Keep a safe copy of your book

Keep your working book outside cloud-synced folders. Back up now makes a local recovery copy; it cannot protect your work if you lose the device.

Open File > Open book > Backups and archives. Choose Create recovery key and keep the key separately from your backups. A lost key means you cannot restore an encrypted archive.

Select Choose backup folder, then Make encrypted archive and save it there. Use a Google Drive or Dropbox synced folder, or a USB drive. You can also copy the archive there afterward. garret remembers the folder; making and saving each archive is manual.

Use Verify encrypted archive to check the saved archive with your key. Restore encrypted archive restores it as a separate book.

Away from your desk

Keep writing on Android.

The Android app gives you a library and scene editor for writing on the go, in light and dark themes.

On desktop, garret also includes series, optional session analytics, and local craft and consistency reports. The interface is available in English and German.
