# Journal style and continuity

Sample editorial material for the first three chapters.

The first three chapters are Jonathan Harker's journal, kept in shorthand. The dated entries are preserved within the scenes; chapter III also begins with an undated continuation. Harker distinguishes observations, deductions, remembered events and uncertainty. His diary headings are dates of writing rather than guarantees that every narrated event occurs on that date. Retain his uncertainty about the encounter with the three women and about the sound from the bag. The timeline uses reading-order scene ordinals so that retrospective narration is not assigned invented elapsed times or a year that this excerpt does not state.
