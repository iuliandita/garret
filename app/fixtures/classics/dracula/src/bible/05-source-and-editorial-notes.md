# Source and sample editorial notes

Sample editorial material for the first three chapters.

Original English prose: Bram Stoker, Dracula, Project Gutenberg ebook 345, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/345 . The downloaded UTF-8 text and complete credits and license are preserved unchanged in source.txt; its credits name Chuck Greif and the Online Distributed Proofreading Team. This manuscript extracts Chapters I, II and III of Jonathan Harker's journal, stopping before Chapter IV. extract.py reproduces the manuscript offline from source.txt. Journal headings and dated entry headings remain in the prose. Added organizational headings split the text at existing diary-entry boundaries, including the undated start of Chapter III. Prose wording, punctuation, paragraph and line breaks, and underscore emphasis are retained; source leading indentation is removed to avoid Markdown code blocks. No illustrations are imported. All synopses, cast descriptions and fields, bible notes, timeline descriptions and scene titles are sample editorial material, not text by Stoker. Appearance rows mark physical presence for characters; people mentioned in memory, letters or conversation receive no such rows.
