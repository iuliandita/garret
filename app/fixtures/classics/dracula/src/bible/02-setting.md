# Setting and movement

Sample editorial material for the first three chapters.

Harker records his route through Munich, Vienna, Buda-Pesth and Klausenburgh before staying at Bistritz. The public coach carries him toward the Borgo Pass, where another driver takes him to the castle. The castle contains an occupied bedroom, dining-room and library as well as locked exits and deserted rooms overlooking a precipice. Carfax near Purfleet is an English property discussed in the library; Harker does not visit it in this excerpt.
