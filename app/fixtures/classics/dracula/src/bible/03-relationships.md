# Relationships and limits of knowledge

Sample editorial material for the first three chapters.

Harker is an English solicitor acting on behalf of Peter Hawkins, and Mina is his fiancee. Neither Hawkins nor Mina physically appears in these chapters. Dracula is Harker's client and host, and Harker comes to believe that he is also his jailer. Harker infers that Dracula was the driver who brought him to the castle, but the arrival scene introduces that person as a driver. The three women are unnamed in the excerpt. Do not supply later identities, family relationships or names from adaptations.
