# Excerpt synopsis

Sample editorial material for the first three chapters.

Jonathan Harker travels to Transylvania for business with Count Dracula. Warnings from local people give way to a frightening night journey and a courteous welcome at the castle. English-language discussions and property arrangements continue as Harker notices that his host has no reflection and that the doors are locked. Discoveries about the Count's movements and the encounter with three women deepen his fear. The excerpt ends when Harker loses consciousness.
