# Source and sample annotations

Pride and Prejudice by Jane Austen, first published in 1813. This sample contains the original English text of chapters 1-3 from Project Gutenberg ebook 1342: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1342.

The retained source.txt includes the Project Gutenberg license and production credits: Chuck Greif and the Online Distributed Proofreading Team, from images at the Internet Archive. Project Gutenberg lists the text as public domain in the USA.

Illustration captions and non-manuscript front matter are omitted. Print line wrapping is joined; emphasis is preserved. Original chapter numbers are retained. Scene labels are added navigation aids.

Cast summaries, synopses, bible notes, and timeline are sample editorial annotations based on these three chapters. They are not part of Austen's manuscript. No modern cover or character art is included.
