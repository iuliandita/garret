# Voice and style sheet

Retain Austen's original spelling, punctuation, dialogue, and emphasis. Do not modernize the manuscript while editing these sample notes.

The narrative moves between ironic general observations, domestic conversation, social report, and a closely overheard exchange. Darcy's fortune and appearance first recommend him; his conduct changes the room's judgment.

Aliases: Elizabeth is Lizzy or Eliza; Catherine is Kitty. Keep aliases with their cast records rather than replacing the names in the prose.

Sample editorial style sheet, not authorial instructions.
