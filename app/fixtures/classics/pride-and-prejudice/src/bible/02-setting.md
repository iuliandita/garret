# Setting and social rules

Longbourn is the Bennets' village; Netherfield Park is Bingley's rented estate. The assembly room brings their circles together. Hertfordshire and London are named in the excerpt.

Introductions, visits, income, and dance partners shape the opening. Mrs. Bennet cannot simply introduce her daughters to the new tenant without the social preliminaries she presses her husband to arrange.

Sample editorial notes based on chapters 1-3. No year or invented estate layout is assigned.
