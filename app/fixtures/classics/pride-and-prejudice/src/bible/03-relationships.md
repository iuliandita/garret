# Family and first impressions

Mr. and Mrs. Bennet are parents to Jane, Elizabeth, Mary, Catherine, and Lydia. Their conversation contrasts his teasing reserve with her urgency about marriage.

Bingley and Darcy are friends. Bingley admires Jane and encourages Darcy to dance; Darcy refuses an introduction to Elizabeth. Elizabeth hears the refusal and tells it as a humorous story.

Miss Bingley and Mrs. Hurst are Bingley's sisters. Mr. Hurst is married to the latter. The excerpt does not yet name both sisters, so these entries retain its forms of address.
