# Book synopsis - opening excerpt

Netherfield's new tenant creates excitement in the Bennet household. Mrs. Bennet sees a possible husband for one of her five daughters, while her husband teases her before revealing that he has already made the necessary visit.

At the assembly, Bingley's easy manners and attention to Jane raise expectations. Darcy's public reserve and private insult leave Elizabeth disposed to laugh at him. The excerpt ends with the two friends having made sharply different impressions.

This synopsis covers chapters 1-3 only. It is a sample editorial annotation, not text by Jane Austen.
