# Continuity checks

Mr. Bennet has visited Bingley before he reveals the visit to his family. At the assembly, Bingley's actual party has five people despite larger numbers in earlier neighborhood reports.

Darcy dances with Mrs. Hurst and Miss Bingley. Bingley dances twice with Jane. Elizabeth is close enough to overhear Darcy declining Bingley's suggested introduction. Mr. Bennet stays home from the assembly.

The timeline uses event order rather than days: these chapters supply no exact calendar dates. Mentioned visits outside the narrated action are not tagged as on-page appearances.
