# Style and continuity

Sample editorial material for the first three chapters.

The narration follows Alice while also commenting on her reasoning. Wordplay, mistaken recitations, conversational misunderstandings and changes in physical scale drive the action. Preserve Carroll's wording and the source's underscore emphasis. Alice drinks before eating the cake; she later shrinks while using the Rabbit's fan. The golden key remains a practical obstacle to entering the garden. The Mouse's tail-shaped poem has its original wording and line breaks here; source indentation is normalized for Markdown, so its visual tail shape is not reproduced.
