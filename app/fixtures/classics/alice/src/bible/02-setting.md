# Setting and movement

Sample editorial material for the first three chapters.

The opening bank belongs to Alice's ordinary surroundings. The rabbit-hole leads through a deep well to a long hall with locked doors, a glass table, and a tiny door opening toward a beautiful garden. Alice sees the garden but does not enter it in these chapters. Her tears form a pool while she is large; after she shrinks, she swims in it and comes ashore with the animals.
