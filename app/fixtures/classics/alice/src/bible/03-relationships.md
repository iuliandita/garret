# Relationships in the excerpt

Sample editorial material for the first three chapters.

Alice's sister reads on the bank and does not accompany her underground. The White Rabbit is preoccupied with his own lateness. Alice tries to converse with the Mouse but repeatedly offends it by mentioning cats and dogs. The Dodo organizes the race, and Alice supplies the prizes. Dinah is Alice's cat, described in conversation and memory; Dinah is not physically present in the excerpt and has no appearance row.
